# ⁠A. Introduction

## Perosnnel Management
- Discipline of hiring and developing employees so that they become more valuable to the organization
- Effective control and use of manpower
- Obtaining, using and maintaining a satisfied workforce
- Also referred as human resource management
- When someone or a team is concerned with the welfare and personnel Management
- Company Policies, staffing, hirin and firing, orientation, wage determination, leave and vacation approval, healthcare programs, training and appraisals

## 3 Aspects of Personnel Managmeent
- The welfare aspect
	- Concerned with cworking conditions and amenities such as canteens, housing, personal problems of workers, therapy
- The Labor/Personnel aspect
	- Concerned with recruitment, placement of employees, remuneration, promotions, incentives
- Concerned with trade union negotations, settlement of industrial disputes, collective bargaining

## Workplace Ergonomics

- Science of designing the workplace
- helps reduce strain, fatigue and injuries by improving the workplace arrangements, reduces health expenses
- as simple as positioning and quality of the chair, working equipments, protective gears, movements during long shifts, lighting conditions
- heavy lifting techniques
- stress management, mental workload
- proper applications of workpace ergonomics can help improve the efficiency of employees and leads to higher productivity
- Research and studies have shown proper application of ergonomics can contribute to improvement of 10-15% productivity.

# ⁠B. Functions of Personal Management
### Managerial Functions
- Planning
- Organizing
- Directing
- Coordinating
- Controlling
### Operative Functions
- Procurement/Employment
	- Hiring of the proper kind and right number of persons necessary to achieve the organizational goals.
	- Process includes determining the manpower requirement in the organization, and recruitment, selection and placement of the newly hired.
- Development
	- After placement of the indivudals, proper education, training and development is an important function of a personnel Management to develop employees efficiency
	- Creates appropriate seminars, training programs and team building projects
- Compensation
	- Determining adequate and fair remuneration of the employees for their contribution
	- Factors involved in determining wage/salary are the requirements of the jobs, basic needs of the employees, minimum wage, ability of an organization to pay, competitors pay, qualifications, experiences.
- Integration
	- Maintaining communication and coordination between the individuals in the organization
	- Develop an effective communication system, program for better integration
	- Harmony between employees and employers, trade unions
- Maintenance
	- Providing training with education is not sufficient, employees must be provided with good and favorable working conditions
	- Personnel Managers should take measure for the health, safety and comfort of the work force
	- Provision of a healthy cafeteria, clean rest rooms, counselling, insurances, recreational facilities, coffee/tea, benefits for employee's family
- Motivation
	- Personnel Managers should work with other departments to develop a system that helps to motivate employees to contribbute as much as they can
	- Various monetary as well as non monetary benefits and incentives can be introduced

# Development of Personal Policy
## Personnel Policty
- Also called as Employee Handbooks
- A statement/document that explains what the emmployer expects from its employees, and what an employee may expect from the employer.
- Policies must be distributed to each employee at the hiring and placement, and should be reviewed and discussed during employee orientation and training sessions by the Personnel Manager
- Personnel Policies often begin with a welcome statements, a brief history of the organization, comapny's values, morale and objectives, and a description of its organization structure
- Policies should also include comprehensive information about workpace rules and regulations that includes hours of work, attendance, pay, benefits, supervision, disciplines, attire
- Should also discuss sensitive legal issues like employee safety regulations, workforce harassment.
