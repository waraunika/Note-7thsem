### Transformation of independent variable
#### For x(t)
1. t +- a -> shifting
2. $\large \dfrac{t}{a}\ \text{or}\ t * a$ -> scaling
3. -t -> Folding / reversal / inversion / mirror image
- transforming independent variable, never changes amplitude.
