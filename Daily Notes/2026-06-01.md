### A.2.3 Circuits
Applying KVL in 2b:
$$\large
v(z+\Delta z, t) - v(z,t) = - R\Delta z\ i(z,t)\ -\ L\Delta z\ \dfrac{\delta i(z,t)}{\delta t}
$$
on dividing by $\Delta$z (unit length) and $\Delta$z -> 0:
$$\large\dfrac{\delta v(z,t)}{\delta t}d = - R\ i(z,t)\ -\ L \dfrac{\delta i(z,t)}{\delta t}
$$
similarly with KCl

For steady state sinusoidal excitation, the voltage and current may be expressed as:
$$\large
v(z,t) = Re[ V(z) e^{jwt}]
$$
$$\large
I(z,t) = Re[ I(z) e^{jwt}]
$$

solving:
$$\large
\begin{align}
\dfrac{dV(z)}{dz} &= - (R+j\omega L)\ I(z)\\
\dfrac{dI(z)}{dz} &= - (G+j\omega C)\ V(z)
\end{align}
$$
solving again
$$\large
\begin{align}
\dfrac{d^2V(z)}{dz^2} &= -\gamma^2\ V(z)\\
\dfrac{d^2I(z)}{dz^2} &= -\gamma^2\ I(z)\\
\text{where,\hspace{2cm}}\\
\gamma\ =\ (\alpha+j\beta)\ &=\ \sqrt{(R+j\omega L)(G + j\omega C)} \hspace{5mm} ...(2.5)
\end{align}
$$
Reflection coefficient at the load is given by the ratio of amplitude of reflected wave to incident
$$\large
\Gamma_l = \dfrac{V_0^-}{V_0^+}
$$
$\gamma$ is complex propagation constant; real part of which gives the attenuation constant $\alpha$ and imaginary part $\beta$. The general solution of 2.5 is:
$$\large
\begin{align}
V(z) &= V_0^+\ e^{-\gamma z}\ + V_0^-\ e^{\gamma z}\\
I(z) &= I_0^+\ e^{-\gamma z}\ + I_0^-\ e^{\gamma z}
\end{align}
$$
-z represent wave propagation in +z direction, towards source
+z represent wave propagation -z direction, towards load

$$\large
\begin{align}
I(z) = \dfrac{1}{Z_0} [V_0^+]
\end{align}
$$
