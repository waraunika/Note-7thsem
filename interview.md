## Organization structure & type

1. What form of ownership is it — sole proprietorship, partnership, or private company?
2. Who holds authority — is there an owner-manager, or a separate manager/supervisor layer?
3. How many levels of management exist (owner → manager → shift lead → staff)?
4. Is the organization structure formal (org chart, defined roles) or informal (everyone does everything)?
5. What's the span of control — how many staff report to one supervisor per shift?

## Departmentalization / internal organization

1. How is work divided — kitchen/BOH, counter/service, cashier, cleaning, procurement?
2. Is there a separation between "back office" tasks (accounts, ordering supplies) and "front" operations?
3. Who handles purchasing and vendor relationships (coffee beans, milk, bakery items)?
4. How is coordination between kitchen and service staff managed during rush hours?

## Planning & management functions

1. How does the cafe plan for daily/weekly demand (staffing, inventory)?
2. What controls exist — cash handling, wastage tracking, quality checks?
Are there written policies (SOPs) or is everything based on the owner's verbal instruction?
How are decisions made — centralized with the owner, or delegated to staff?

4. Motivation (directly from your book's 6.1 framework)

What motivates staff to perform well — pay, tips, recognition, flexible hours?
Does management use any incentive system (bonuses, tip-sharing, employee-of-the-month)?
How does the cafe handle discipline — verbal warning, written warning, termination?
Which motivation theory seems to fit best here — Maslow, Herzberg, or McGregor's Theory X/Y? Does management treat staff as needing supervision (X) or as self-motivated (Y)?
Is there any job enrichment — do staff rotate/promote roles or are they stuck in one task indefinitely?

5. Personnel/HR management (6.2 framework)

How does the cafe recruit staff — referrals, walk-ins, ads?
Is there any job analysis or job description, or is hiring informal?
How are wages structured — fixed salary, hourly, tips-based, or mixed?
Is there any training given to new staff, or is it learn-on-the-job?
Are performance appraisals done at all, even informally?
What's the staff turnover rate, and why do people usually leave?

6. Organizational behaviour (6.3 framework)

How do staff interact — is there visible teamwork or more individual silos?
What's the communication pattern — direct instructions from owner, or peer-to-peer coordination?
How is conflict handled (e.g., disputes over shifts, mistakes, customer complaints)?
Does staff morale visibly affect service quality during a busy period?
